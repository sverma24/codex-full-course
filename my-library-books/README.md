# My Library Books

A tiny page for keeping track of the books you've borrowed from Fernhollow Public Library:
when each one is due, which ones are late, and how much you owe.

Open `index.html` in your browser. That's it. There's no server, no database and nothing to install.
Your books are saved in your browser.

## The library's rules

- Late fees are **25 cents a day**, starting the day **after** the due date.
- A book never costs more than **$5.00** in late fees.
- The list shows the book that's due **soonest at the top**.
